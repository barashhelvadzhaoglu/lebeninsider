One of the things I like most about life here is the wide open spaces and greenery between buildings.

## Our building

The building we live in has 8 floors with 6 flats on each, so roughly 50 flats in one block. Even so, there are lovely tall trees in front of it. Every building has space for bicycles. Each flat has a storage room in the basement, a **Keller**. Many buildings also have playgrounds and sandpits in front of them.

In other words, the city is spread out over a much larger area.

## The price: everything is a bit further away

This has a price. When everything is spacious and green, the supermarket or the small shops you need end up further away, and getting there is not always easy.

When I lived in Istanbul, there was a Şok supermarket in the building next door, a BİM in the building beside that, and an A101 across the street. Three supermarkets within 50 metres. The city is squeezed into a small space.

Here you have to walk at least 500 metres to a kilometre just to get to the supermarket. That is why a bike or a car is almost a necessity. People do their shopping and carry it home on their bikes or in their backpacks.

## Renting

Then there is renting: Germany has laws that protect tenants very strongly. That is another thing that feels different for someone coming from Turkey.

## Which suits you?

A dense city where everything is on your doorstep, or a green, spacious city where every errand takes a little travel? For me, green space and my children's safety weigh more. For you that may not be the case.
