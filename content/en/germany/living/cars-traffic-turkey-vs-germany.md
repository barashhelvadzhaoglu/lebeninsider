Getting around is one of the things that has made my life in Germany easiest.

## The Deutschlandticket

Here there is the Deutschlandticket: you pay 63 euros a month and can use all public transport for the whole month. Only high-speed trains (like the ICE) cost extra. You can buy monthly passes in Turkey too, but it is not like this. From what I have seen, getting around here is very easy. I can just get on a bus and go.

## Being able to walk with a stroller

This was one of my problems in Turkey: I could not walk on the street with a stroller. Cars are constantly parked on the corners of pavements and block the footpaths.

Here I can walk around easily with a stroller. And I don't just mean public transport, tickets, buses and trains; all the streets and pavements are planned properly and comfortably.

## A city for people with disabilities

The same ease applies to people with disabilities. Life with a disability is much easier here. People in wheelchairs can go out and get around easily. There are lifts everywhere, and pavements and footpaths are built with this in mind.

## Cycling

There are bike lanes all over the city; you can get around easily by bike, with a stroller or in a wheelchair.

I love cycling. I deliver orders by bike for Lieferando, I commute by bike, and I can get anywhere in the city easily.

As far as I can see, the metro is the fastest way to get around. But if your start and end points are not close to a metro station, a bike is usually faster than dealing with transfers. Buses tend to be slow. With a bike you are fast, you don't get stuck in traffic, and it isn't tiring.

## Cargo bikes

Many families here buy electric or three-wheeled bikes and put their children in a seat at the front. These are called **cargo bikes**. With them, families can carry their children easily without dealing with traffic.

## In short

In Turkey, the car is at the centre of life. Here the city is planned around people on foot, on bikes, with strollers and in wheelchairs. For someone like me who loves cycling and has young children, that is a huge difference.
