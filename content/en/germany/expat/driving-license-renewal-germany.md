One of the first questions people moving from Turkey to Germany ask is: "How long can I drive here with my Turkish licence?" The short answer: **six months from your residence registration.** After that you need a German licence, and for a Turkish licence that means tests. Here is the rule and the process.

*Note: Based on my own experience and the official rules; not legal advice. Check with your local driving licence office (Führerscheinstelle) for current information.*

## The six-month rule

From the moment you start living in Germany, that is, from your residence registration (**Anmeldung**), your Turkish licence is valid for **six months**. If you can show you will stay in Germany for less than twelve months in total (for example with a fixed-term work contract), you can ask for a six-month extension. If you live here permanently, you need to switch to a German licence within that time.

Six months goes by faster than you think, and the process can take several months. Start as soon as you move.

## Converting a Turkish licence to a German one

Turkey is not on Germany's list of countries whose licences can be exchanged without tests. So you need:

1. **Eye test** (Sehtest): available at opticians.
2. **First-aid course** (Erste-Hilfe-Kurs): a one-day course.
3. **Application:** at the licensing office with your passport, residence document, Turkish licence and its German translation, biometric photo, eye test and first-aid certificates.
4. **Theory test:** multiple choice on a computer. Turkish is available as a language.
5. **Practical test:** the test car is usually provided by a driving school, so in practice you need to sign up with one.

There is no legal minimum number of lessons, but a few lessons to get used to German traffic rules help most people. Total costs depend on the driving school and how many lessons you take.

## If you have an EU licence: the rules changed in August 2026

If you exchanged your Turkish licence for an EU licence in another EU country (for example Bulgaria), Germany has recognised that licence since 18 August 2026 and exchanges it for a German one **without tests**. That is how I got mine; I describe it in my [Bulgarian licence story](/en/germany/expat/bulgarian-license-valid-in-germany/).

## Documents you will need

Requirements vary slightly between offices, but usually:

- Passport and residence document
- Residence registration certificate (Anmeldebescheinigung)
- Your original Turkish licence
- A **German translation** of the licence, from a sworn translator or an authorised organisation such as ADAC
- Biometric photo
- Eye test certificate (Sehtestbescheinigung)
- First-aid course certificate

## Costs

- **Application fee:** depends on the office, usually around €40–50.
- **Theory test:** about €25 (testing organisation fee).
- **Practical test:** about €130 (testing organisation fee).
- **Eye test, first-aid course, translation:** a few tens of euros each.
- **Driving school:** the biggest cost. Registration fee, number of lessons and the school's fee for presenting you at the test vary widely. Get quotes from several schools.

Test fees are set officially; driving school prices are not.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does an International Driving Permit extend the six months?**
No. It only serves as a translation of your Turkish licence; it doesn't change the six-month limit.

**What happens if I keep driving on my Turkish licence after six months?**
In Germany that counts as driving without a licence, which is a criminal offence, and your insurance may refuse to pay after an accident. Apply before the deadline.

**Do I have to hand in my Turkish licence?**
Yes. You usually hand it in when you receive your German licence.

**Can I take the theory test in Turkish?**
Yes, Turkish is one of the available languages.

**Do I have to go to a driving school?**
There is no legal minimum number of lessons. But the practical test needs a test car and an instructor, so in practice you sign up with a school.

## In short

- A Turkish licence is valid in Germany for **six months from Anmeldung**.
- If you live here permanently, switch to a German licence in that time with an **eye test, first aid, theory and practical tests**.
- If your licence was exchanged in another EU country, since August 2026 you can exchange it without tests.
