When it comes to choosing a profession, a topic that isn't talked about much in Turkey also comes into play: **financial literacy**. In my opinion, children and young people need to learn this at an early age.

*Note: I am sharing my own thoughts in this post. I am not a financial advisor, and this post is not investment advice.*

## What financial literacy is not

What I mean by financial literacy is not: "Let me buy crypto, day trade constantly on the stock market, set up trading software in front of screens, and quickly make big money." That is not what I am talking about.

## What do I mean?

There are various investment vehicles such as stocks, funds, gold, and real estate. What matters is knowing and learning things like:

- What are the dynamics of these investment vehicles, and how do they work?
- During which periods do certain instruments yield gains, and when do they cause losses?
- For example, does buying a house make sense from a ten-year perspective?
- In times of rising interest rates, might it be necessary to avoid certain higher-risk instruments?

In other words, knowing how to evaluate different investment vehicles. People learn this over time by looking back at historical patterns. But the earlier it is learned, the better.

## Regular savings at a young age

Here is my recommendation: Young people usually live with their families when they are young, and basic expenses like food are either non-existent or minimal. During this period, while working, they should set aside a portion of their money and build **regular savings**. And they should invest these savings into sound, sensible, and reliable investment vehicles.

Just think: someone starts investing regularly at age 20. By the time they turn 40, they might not even have to work, because the investments they made could generate enough regular return to sustain their life.

One of the key points here is this: if you save regularly from a young age and choose investment vehicles that can provide **regular income**, such as dividends, you can achieve financial peace of mind in 10–15 years.

## A book recommendation

There is a well-known book on this topic: **The Richest Man in Babylon**. The book explains how setting aside ten percent of your income and investing it properly can lead to significant wealth over the years. It is well worth reading in detail.
