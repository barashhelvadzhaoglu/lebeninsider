*This post is part of the "Before Choosing a Profession" series I created on career choice and planning. All parts of the series: [Career](/tr/kariyer/)*

The skills and certifications you acquire at the beginning of your career are crucial for your future life.

I watched a video on YouTube by Serkan Ünal about this, and I highly recommend it to young people. What he explained was this: at the start of your career, you could work a steady job with comfortable conditions and perhaps a better salary compared to your friends. But then your future growth remains within certain standard limits. If you work harder at the start of your career, putting in more effort than your peers to gain more skills, certifications, and competencies, these will pay off in the later stages of your career in terms of salary and position.

That's why it is essential to make the most of this early period.

## My choice

Personally, I chose to take this path. Back in the day, my friends were working at the largest companies in the industry, doing more comfortable jobs. I, on the other hand, put in more effort at a smaller company and earned various skills and certifications. Along my career journey, I received job offers from some of Turkey's biggest banks, but I stayed on my own path. In the end, I believe I reached higher-level positions than my friends.

## My manager's story

One of my managers experienced a similar situation. He previously worked at one of Turkey's largest companies, then moved to the company where I was working. Looking back today, he holds a great position in a major global company, not just in Turkey. His friends from that time may have also reached important positions at top companies in Turkey, but they couldn't reach the level he attained.

## Competencies you can build at the start of your career

Getting a certification can happen in many ways:

- **Professional certifications:** To give an example from my field, like CCNA. Or teaching-related certifications.
- **Language certifications:** Like TOEFL for English.
- **Course certificates:** Taking a course and earning a certificate.
- **Self-preparation:** Studying on Udemy, preparing for and taking an exam to get certified, or simply completing a few courses on Udemy.

## Life is not equal for everyone

Of course, when we discuss these topics, we are thinking about somewhat ideal scenarios. Someone might pull strings, or some people get shortcuts. Unfortunately, life is not equal and fair for everyone. Not everyone gets an education at private colleges. Not everyone finishes elementary, middle, and high school starting out with a certain level of English or having received a solid foundational education.

In that case, you have to develop your own skills. Learning a foreign language is one of them, which I will cover separately in the next section.
