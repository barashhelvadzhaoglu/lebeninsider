As a second step, I recommend preparing a report on the **CV format**. Because expectations regarding CV formats can vary across countries, and even across different industries.

## Questions to research

For example, if you are targeting Germany:

- What do companies in Germany expect from a CV format?
- Should you include a photo in your CV?
- Is using a fancy CV design an advantage or a disadvantage?
- What sections should be in the CV?
- In what order should these sections be placed?
- What information should you include in your CV? Marital status, hobbies, etc.—which of these belong on a CV?

## Again, different tools, different reports

I recommend having these prepared as reports using deep research mode as well. As I mentioned, if you gather reports from different AI tools, you can then evaluate them side by side later on. There will be differences between the reports, and those differences will show you what you really need to pay attention to.
