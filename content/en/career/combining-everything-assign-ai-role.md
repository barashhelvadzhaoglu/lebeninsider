Final step: AI tools can blend all these reports and your own story together to create a solid CV. In my opinion, this is currently the most convenient and sensible way to build a CV.

## Assign a role to AI

The AI tools you use here and how you instruct them are important. You need to learn how to collaborate with AI tools.

AI models generally behave like a general intelligence with a generic persona. But if you **assign them a role** like "You are an expert CV consultant," they act accordingly. The results you get change significantly based on this.

## Which tool for which task?

My personal preference is as follows: preparing the reports using tools like Google Gemini or ChatGPT in deep research mode. For the task of combining all these reports and drafting the CV, I prefer Claude. The reports prepared by Claude can certainly be used as well, but when it comes to bringing all the reports together and evaluating them, I think it's the best.

## Ready-to-use prompt

You can copy the text below and customize the bracketed parts for yourself. Then, add the reports you prepared in the previous parts along with your own narrative:

```
You are an experienced CV consultant who knows the [Germany / Istanbul] job market
well and has recruited in the field of [network engineering].

I will provide you with three types of documents:
1. Research reports on the expectations of companies in the target market for this position
2. Reports on the CV format in this country (photo, headings, order, which personal details)
3. My own narrative: my experiences, projects, products/software I used,
   trainings I received, and my personal attributes

Your task:
- Point out contradictions between reports and explain which one is more reliable.
- Prioritize the information in my narrative according to the target market's expectations.
- Prepare a one-page CV in [English / German] following the format recommended in the reports.
- Do not add any experience, certification, or skill that is not in my narrative.
- Ask me questions about information you find missing that could strengthen the CV.
```

## A word of caution

The last two points in the prompt are there on purpose. To make the CV stronger, AI tools can sometimes add experience or skills you don't actually have. Whatever is written on your CV, they will ask you about it in the interview. You have to be able to stand behind everything you write. Make sure to check the CV generated by AI line by line before submitting it.
