Writing a CV is the hardest part of job hunting for many people: you don't know what to write, how to write it, or what to highlight. In my opinion, the most sensible way to do this today is to use AI tools properly. But simply saying "write me a CV" isn't enough; first, you need to provide the AI with the right material.

In this guide, I explain the method I use in four steps. I give examples from my own profession, network engineering; you can adapt them to your own field.

## 1. First, the target: Which city, which market?

The first step is choosing the target region and city. For example:

- I live in Turkey, but I want to move to Germany.
- Or I live in Turkey, and I want to work in Istanbul.

This target must be established first, because every market has different expectations. A CV sent to Munich is not the same as a CV sent to Istanbul.

Next, I recommend generating reports by using AI tools in **deep research** mode. Get **different reports from different AI tools** on the same topic. Here are questions you can ask:

- What do companies in Istanbul (or Germany) expect from network engineers?
- What qualifications and traits should network engineers have?
- What specific skills are companies in this market looking for?
- What elements on a CV attract attention?

These reports will form the foundation of what you need to highlight on your CV. If you don't yet know which city to target, I explained how I chose my LinkedIn location in [the first step to moving to Germany](/en/germany/expat/first-step-to-move-to-germany/).

## 2. Format research: Should you include a photo?

The second step is preparing a report on the **CV format**. Expectations for CV formatting can vary across different countries and even across industries. For example, if you are targeting Germany, research the following:

- What do companies in Germany expect from a CV format (Lebenslauf)?
- Should you include a photo on your CV?
- Are fancy, heavily designed CV templates an advantage or a disadvantage?
- What sections should be included in the CV, and in what order?
- Which personal details—such as marital status or hobbies—should appear on the CV?

Have different AI tools prepare reports on these questions in deep research mode as well. Differences will emerge between the reports; these discrepancies will show you exactly what to pay close attention to.

## 3. Describe yourself out loud

In my opinion, this is the most critical step. We often describe our work too simply, which doesn't translate well onto a CV; writing it down can also feel daunting. My recommendation: just like I do when preparing these blog posts, **speak out loud** and transcribe your voice into text. The voice-to-text dictation feature on your phone is more than enough for this.

**If you are experienced**, describe your daily work to the AI in long, detailed descriptions:

- Which projects have you been involved in?
- What specific tasks did you handle?
- Which products, product families, and software do you work with?

**If you are a recent graduate**, describe your personal strengths:

> I really enjoy working with a team. I usually handle the organization with my friends—I pick where we go, and I handle the planning.

Then explain your passion for the field:

> I decided to become a network engineer. That's why I took courses on Udemy and studied them thoroughly. I couldn't take the certification exam due to budget constraints, but once I start working, I can take the exam within a few months.

You can speak in a natural, conversational tone. Your sentences don't need to be polished; what matters is getting everything down. I also explained what I look for in a recent graduate as a hiring manager in [a separate post](/en/career/what-i-expect-from-fresh-graduates/).

## 4. Bring it all together: Assign a role to the AI

In the final step, the AI can blend all these research reports and your self-description to produce a solid CV. The tool you use and the instructions you provide are crucial here.

By default, AI models act with a "generic persona." But if you **assign it a role** by saying "You are an expert CV consultant," it behaves according to that persona, and the quality of the output changes significantly.

My personal preference is this: I generate the reports using tools like Google Gemini or ChatGPT in deep research mode, and then I use Claude to combine all the reports and draft the CV. In my experience, Claude is the best when it comes to synthesizing multiple reports and evaluating them critically.

### Ready-to-use prompt

Copy the text below, customize the bracketed fields for yourself, and then paste your generated reports along with your personal transcript:

```
You are an experienced CV consultant who knows the [Germany / Istanbul]
job market well and has recruited in the field of [network engineering].

I will provide you with three types of documents:
1. Research reports on what companies in the target market expect from this position
2. Reports on the CV format in this country (photo, sections, ordering, personal details)
3. My own background notes: my experiences, projects, products/software used,
   trainings received, and personal attributes

Your task:
- Point out any contradictions between the reports and explain which recommendation is more reliable.
- Prioritize the details from my background based on the target market's expectations.
- Prepare a one-page CV in [English / German] following the format recommended in the reports.
- Do not invent or add any experience, certification, or skill that is not mentioned in my notes.
- If you notice missing details that would strengthen the CV, ask me about them as follow-up questions.
```

### A word of caution

The last two instructions in the prompt are there intentionally. AI tools sometimes add skills or experiences you don't actually have just to make the CV look stronger. Whatever is written on your CV, interviewers will ask you about it; you need to be able to stand behind every single word. Always review the AI-generated CV line by line before submitting it anywhere.

## In summary

1. Choose your target market and research its expectations.
2. Research the standard CV format for that market.
3. Describe yourself thoroughly by speaking out loud.
4. Have an AI model with an assigned role synthesize everything, and verify the final result yourself.
