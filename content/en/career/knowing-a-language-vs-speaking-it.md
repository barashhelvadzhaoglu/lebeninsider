Learning English is no longer something you can afford to skip. Anyone aiming for a career abroad, in particular, absolutely must master English.

## A second language depending on your goal

Alongside English, other languages are also needed depending on your goals:

- If you want to live in Germany, Switzerland, or Austria, you need to learn **German**.
- **French** opens doors to a massive market.
- **Spanish** is the second most spoken language in the world.
- Learning languages spoken by huge populations, like **Russian** and **Chinese**, can also vary depending on your career goals.

However, learning these languages takes serious effort.

## A certificate is required...

This isn't just about languages. People at the start of their careers have to prove their knowledge by earning various certifications—whether Cisco certifications or teaching credentials. The industry demands this. That’s why anyone who claims "I know this language" also definitely needs a certificate to back it up.

## ...but a certificate isn't enough either

Unfortunately, just passing the exam isn't enough. You are also expected to **speak** that language.

Speaking from my own experience: passing an exam in a language is one thing; actually speaking that language is something entirely different. You can study for an exam, pass it, and say, "I hold a B1 level certificate." But when someone says, "Let's hold this meeting in English," you might struggle. There are many situations like this, and these nuances matter.

## How should you learn?

There are many methods. My recommendation is **repetition**.

Constantly watching series and movies in that language will definitely pay off when learning it. In particular, watch the shows and movies you love over and over again. Even if you understand nothing the first time you watch, by the third or fifth time, you will inevitably pick up certain words and idioms.

It won't click right away. But when you do the same thing over and over, after the fifth or tenth repetition, it will click. You just have to keep working at it without giving up.
