*This post is the first part of my "Before Choosing a Career" series on career choice and career planning. All parts of the series: [Career](/tr/kariyer/)*

When choosing a career, most of us look at the same questions: How in demand will this profession be in the future? Is it easy to find a job in this field? How much money will I make?

These are important questions, and I'll touch on all of them one by one in this series. But in my opinion, the real problem is that these questions are the **first** ones asked.

## We Choose the Career and Try to Fit Ourselves into It

From what I've seen, at least in Turkey, people usually choose a career first and then try to adapt themselves to it. Maybe it's similar in other countries too, I wouldn't know.

We make decisions based on whether a job "pays better" or is "in higher demand." In my view, this is the wrong approach.

First, you need to know yourself. What do you love? What do you enjoy doing? How do you want to spend your time? Once you figure these out, you need to look at professions through this lens. In other words, getting to know yourself first and then turning what you love into your career makes much more sense.

## It's Not Easy, Which Is Why You Need to Plan

Of course, this isn't easy. The answer to "What do I enjoy doing?" doesn't become clear overnight. You need to plan for it.

The first step is to start by knowing yourself: clarifying what you like doing and what brings you joy.

## Build Your Profile

So how do you get to know yourself? My suggestion is simple: before starting your research, **build your own profile**.

### Take Notes for Yourself

Whenever you catch yourself enjoying something during the day, jot it down somewhere. No need for elaborate sentences. For example:

> I enjoy going out with my friends. I make the plans for where we go, I pick the restaurant. I organize who's coming, where, and how we meet. I really enjoy doing these things.

On its own, this might seem like a small note. But accumulated over weeks, these notes will say a lot about you. Do you like organizing, fixing things, talking to people, or immersing yourself in a problem all on your own?

It doesn't matter where you keep the notes. A notebook, the notes app on your phone, whatever feels comfortable.

### Analyze the Notes with AI

If you share these notes regularly with AI tools—for instance, at the end of the day—a profile will emerge before you. The more notes you have, the clearer that profile will become.

From this profile, you can do two things:

- You can try to **match** your career research on the internet with this profile.
- Or you can use AI tools again to generate a **list of professions that fit your profile**.

This list isn't a final decision; it's a starting point. You still need to do thorough research for every profession on that list. I'll explain how you can do that in the upcoming parts of this series.

## It's Never Too Late for Anything

In my view, the most critical point here is this: choosing a job that fits your personality, your own profile, and planning it properly.

And it's never too late for this. People can change careers and jobs at any stage of their lives. I know it isn't easy. But it's not impossible either. Many people have pulled it off. Why shouldn't you?
