My advice to young people on this topic is this: Gather information using today's AI tools. But instead of just telling these tools "I want this," conduct real research with them.

## Use deep research mode

When doing research, be sure to use **deep research** and web browsing features. This feature scours numerous sources across the internet and compiles a comprehensive report for you. It's usually limited—often to something like once per day on free tiers. That's why breaking your research down across several days makes a lot of sense.

## Day-by-day plan

- **Day 1:** Which professions will be needed most in the future?
- **Day 2:** Which professions will earn the most money in the future?
- **Day 3:** Which professions will be in the highest demand in the future?
- **Day 4:** Workers in which professions will enjoy more comfortable living conditions in the future?

By combining the findings of these 3–4 reports, you can identify **5 to 10 professions** that stick with you and make sense.

Also explore the same topics with **different AI tools**. Each tool may look at different sources and come up with different conclusions. Review these reports thoroughly and carefully.

## Then, conduct separate research for each profession

You can conduct a new inquiry each day for the 5–10 professions you have selected. For instance, let's say one of the professions is agricultural engineering:

- What does a day, a week, or a month look like for an agricultural engineer?
- What are the responsibilities of an agricultural engineer?
- In which fields can an agricultural engineer work?
- What traits and qualities are required to do this job?

You can dive even deeper: How much do agricultural engineers earn in Germany? How much in the Netherlands? What is the standard of living for agricultural engineers in the US? You can have AI tools generate these kinds of detailed reports and analyses for you.

I also recommend putting together a template showing how someone in that profession spends their day—how much of their daily 8 hours goes where. You can research and evaluate the handful of professions that appeal to you from this perspective.

## Research unfamiliar terms too

Since you are currently an outsider to these professions, you will inevitably come across unfamiliar terms and expressions. You should look those up as well to make them clearer and more concrete. You cannot make a sound decision about a profession without truly understanding it.
