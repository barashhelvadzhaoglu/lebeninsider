In the first part of the series, I mentioned that you need to know yourself first before choosing a career. But how do you get to know yourself? The answer to "What do I love doing?" doesn't show up overnight.

My suggestion is simple: **build your own profile** before you start researching.

## Take notes for yourself

Whenever you notice what you enjoy doing throughout the day, write it down somewhere. There is no need for elaborate sentences. For example:

> I like going out with my friends. I make the plans for where we'll go, I pick the restaurant. I organize who will come, where and how we'll meet. I enjoy doing these things.

This might seem like a small note on its own. But accumulated over weeks, these notes will reveal a lot about you. Do you like organizing, fixing things, talking to people, or getting deeply absorbed in a problem on your own?

It doesn't matter where you keep these notes. A notebook, the notes app on your phone, whatever feels easiest.

## Evaluate the notes with AI

If you share these notes regularly with AI tools—say, at the end of the day—a profile will emerge. The more notes you gather, the clearer that profile will become.

You can do two things based on this profile:

- You can try to **match** your online career research against this profile.
- Or, again using AI tools, you can generate a **list of careers suited to your profile**.

This list is not a final decision; it is a starting point. You still need to do detailed research for each profession on the list. I'll explain how to do that in the upcoming parts of the series.
