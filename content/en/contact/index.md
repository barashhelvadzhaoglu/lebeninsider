## When to write to me

**To share your experience**
If you have been through a similar process in Germany or elsewhere in Europe – with a different outcome or your own story – I would love to hear from you. This blog is one person's notes, and readers' experiences are what make the articles truly useful.

**About an IT career in Germany**
If you want to build a career in networking, security or IT in general in Germany, are looking for a job, or are just getting started in the field, send me your questions. I'm happy to share my own experience with interviews, CVs, certifications, landing a first job and career planning.

**For corrections or updates**
Laws change and procedures get updated. If you spot information that is wrong or outdated, please let me know and I'll fix it.

**For collaborations**
If you have an idea for a content collaboration, a guest post or another project about Germany, Europe, travel or IT careers, feel free to write.

**Just to ask a question**
If you have a question about German bureaucracy, moving or planning a trip: if I know the answer, I'll share it; if I don't, I'll tell you honestly.
