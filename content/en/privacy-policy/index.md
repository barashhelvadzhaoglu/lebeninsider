## 2. General

This website is a private information site about life in Germany, travel and careers. There are no user accounts, no comment function and no newsletter. Personal data is only processed as described below.

## 3. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub processes technically necessary data (e.g. IP address, date and time, requested page, browser information) in server log files to deliver the website and keep it secure.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably). Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which GitHub is certified. More: [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 4. Content delivery network (Cloudflare)

We use Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) to deliver and protect the website. Requests are routed through Cloudflare servers, which process technically necessary data such as IP address and request data.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More: [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## 5. Advertising (Google AdSense) and consent

This website uses Google AdSense, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"), to show ads. Google may use cookies and similar technologies and process data such as IP address, device and browser information and information about your use of this website to deliver, personalise and measure ads.

On your first visit, a consent banner (Google's consent management platform) asks whether you agree to personalised advertising and the use of cookies. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. Without consent, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown where possible. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time via the privacy and cookie settings button provided by the consent banner.

Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More:
- [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Advertising at Google](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

## 6. Contact by email

If you email us, we process your email address and message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete the data once it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods apply.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For private providers in Bavaria this is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, [www.lda.bayern.de](https://www.lda.bayern.de).
