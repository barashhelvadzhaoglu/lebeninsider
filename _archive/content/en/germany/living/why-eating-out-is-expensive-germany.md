Eating out in Germany, especially in Munich, is generally expensive. You realize this best when you compare it to supermarket prices.

## The difference between supermarkets and restaurants

In the supermarket, prices look roughly like this:

- **1 kg minced meat:** 10–12 euros
- **1 kg chicken breast:** 8–10 euros

Yet even the smallest McDonald's burger menu is roughly around these prices. In other words, for the cost of a single fast-food meal for one person, you can buy enough meat for several family meals.

## Even the cheapest options outside are around 10 euros

- Even at places considered cheap, like Asian takeout spots, menus start at 10 euros.
- A plain Margherita pizza also starts at 10 euros.

When you eat out as a family, the bill adds up very quickly.

## Conclusion: bringing food along

That is why people end up bringing their own lunch to work. When heading to parks, they also bring food, sandwiches, and similar items along. This is completely normal here; nobody finds it odd.

*Note: Prices are based on my own observations; they vary depending on the city, supermarket, and time period.*
