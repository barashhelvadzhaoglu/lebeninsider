In my opinion, the third part is the most important: explaining yourself.

Since we usually explain what we do in very simple terms, it doesn't turn out well suited for a CV. Writing it down can also feel hard. Here is my suggestion: **explain it by speaking**, just like I'm doing right now, and convert your speech into text. The voice-to-text feature on your phone is more than enough for this.

## If You Have Experience

As I mentioned in the other posts, explain what you do in your daily life to AI tools at length and in detail:

- Which projects were you involved in?
- What kind of tasks did you do?
- Which products, product groups, or software do you work with?

## If You Are a Fresh Graduate

If you are a fresh graduate, you can describe your personal traits as I mentioned earlier. For example:

> I really love working in a team. I organize events with my friends, I decide where we go, and I handle the planning.

Then explain your interest:

> I decided to become a network engineer. That's why I took courses on Udemy and studied them. Since my budget wasn't available, I couldn't take the exam, but if I start working, I can take the exam within a few months.

You can convey all of this to AI tools in a normal conversational tone. Your sentences don't need to be perfect; what matters is that you explain everything.
