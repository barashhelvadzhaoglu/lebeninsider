As a continuation of my career posts, I want to prepare a guide on writing a CV. I will break it down into four parts. This is the first part.

## First, determine the target region

The first step is to choose the target region and city. For example:

- I live in Turkey, but I want to move to Germany.
- Or I live in Turkey and want to work in Istanbul.

This goal needs to be determined first, because every market has different expectations.

## Research expectations in that market

Next, I recommend generating reports in **deep research** mode using AI tools once again. And getting **different reports from different AI tools** on the same topic.

Questions you could ask (again, I am giving network engineering as an example; everyone can customize it for their own profession):

- What do companies in Istanbul expect from network engineers?
- What qualities and skills should network engineers have?
- What qualifications are companies in Istanbul looking for? Or what qualities do companies in Germany look for in network engineers?
- Which qualifications catch recruiters' attention when listed on a CV?

Several different reports can be prepared using these questions. These reports will form the foundation of what you need to highlight on your CV.
